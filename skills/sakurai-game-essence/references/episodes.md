## B01 — Risk and Reward
*B: Game Essence · 10:00 · [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FXqEykD5Ub4)*

**Technique:** Co-locating Maximum Risk with Attack Opportunities — Core offensive and rewarding actions should peak in efficacy precisely at the spatial and temporal point of maximum player vulnerability. Forcing the player to enter direct collision or danger zones to score a hit—such as stomping an oncoming enemy or positioning directly beneath descending targets—
- ✅ Align the execution window of offensive moves with the exact point of enemy lethal contact (e.g., Mario jumping on a Koopa at point-blank proximity).
- ✅ Structure secondary rewards (like kicking a shell) to introduce fresh reciprocal risks (rebounding shells that can damage the player).
- ✅ Ensure safe states offer zero offensive progress, forcing players to willingly leave cover to engage.
- ❌ Allowing players to safely neutralize threats from total distance without exposure to reciprocal danger, which trivializes the tension.
- ❌ Overpowering player arsenals (like an unconstrained 3-way spread laser) that wipe out threats without demanding alignment or risk-taking.
**Technique:** Directional Interception Trajectory Design — When moving threats fire projectiles while traversing, their true hazard footprint becomes diagonal rather than purely vertical. Systematically designing enemy trajectories so that pursuing from behind yields high risk with zero reward, while intercepting head-on yields identical risk with guarantee
- ✅ Factor projectile travel speed and emitter lateral velocity together to calculate true danger corridors.
- ✅ Design movement patterns so that chasing targets places players into bomb streams without direct line-of-fire.
- ✅ Reward proactive frontal interception where risk exposure guarantees target alignment.
- ❌ Symmetric threat profiles where chasing and intercepting produce identical reward opportunities, removing spatial ingenuity.
**Technique:** Inverse Balancing of Game Essence and Broad Appeal — Deepening a game's 'essence' through demanding risk-and-reward balancing naturally raises tension, which simultaneously reduces broad casual appeal. Designers aiming for high accessibility must deliberately attenuate strict risk/reward penalties and simplify mechanics to welcome wider demographics.
- ✅ Evaluate intended audience before tuning punishment thresholds and strict risk-reward trade-offs.
- ✅ Dial down mandatory high-stakes risk when onboarding broad or younger demographics (as seen in Kirby's Dream Land).
- ✅ Allow low-risk alternative paths or forgiving buffer states when aiming for broad market penetration.
- ❌ Assuming maximum mechanical depth and intense push-and-pull is universally desirable for every player demographic.

- ✅ Tie difficulty directly to high-stakes currency bets rather than static menu toggles to make balancing an active gameplay choice.
- ✅ Scale tangible rewards (loot drop rates, rare weapons, score multipliers) proportionally to the chosen difficulty level.
- ✅ Allow difficulty to be adjusted per level or encounter rather than locking an entire playthrough into a single difficulty mode.
- ❌ Locking players into a global difficulty setting from start to finish where lowering it permanently tarnishes the save file.
- ❌ Applying currency bets to systems where the earned currency has no essential functional value, diluting the perceived stakes.
**Technique:** Dignified Automatic Failure Downscaling — When a player fails an encounter, automatically step down the difficulty while penalizing a portion of their wagered stake rather than bluntly prompting them with a humiliating 'Switch to Easy Mode?' dialogue. This prevents players from getting hard-stuck while preserving their dignity and maintaini
- ✅ Automatically reduce difficulty by a fractional increment upon death/retry so the player naturally progresses without hitting an impassable wall.
- ✅ Spill or forfeit a portion of the player's wagered stakes upon defeat to maintain consequence without halting gameplay entirely.
- ✅ Avoid patronizing modal popups asking struggling players to switch to an easy difficulty.
- ❌ Halting failure flow with an explicit 'Do you want to switch to Easy Mode?' prompt that demoralizes struggling players.
- ❌ Hiding or under-explaining the mechanism to lower difficulty below baseline, leaving beginners trapped in standard difficulty.
